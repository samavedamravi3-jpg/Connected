# Product Brief

## Promise
Find someone nearby who can help you take your next career step, and who is willing to help.

## Audience
Students, early-career professionals, career changers, mentors, and hackathon attendees. The first useful conversation is the success metric, not follower count.

## First version
1. Four-step preference onboarding: goal, identity/field, help offered, privacy.
2. Map with company labels, people, events, and illustrative community hotspots.
3. Search and filters for industry, company, distance, and availability.
4. Evidence-based match reasons using explicitly offered help.
5. Editable icebreaker drafts, chat requests, accept/decline/cancel, saved people/events.
6. Public-venue meetup creation and expiring discovery.

## Demo scope
NYC/Flatiron sample data. All example names, employer affiliations, activity counts, and events are fictional. Live community mode uses consented profiles and user-created events only.

## Future scope
Verified employers, real event provider integration, full messaging, organizer tools, user reports and moderation, proximity queries with pagination, email/password auth for independent hosting, consent-based introduction graph.
