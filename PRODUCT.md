## Users

College students and young professionals looking for mentorship, advice, peers and collaborators nearby.

## Product Purpose

Professional networking with map-first discovery: see relevant people and events around you, open a profile, send a connection request, and chat once accepted.

## Operating Context

Used on a phone, often on the move (campus, events, cafes). Four tabs; the map with a bottom sheet of people is the home surface. Surface mode: Operate.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Map with clusters, hotspots (Major hub / Active hub / Emerging spot), filters, layers and a 3-position bottom sheet.
- Events list, inbox (sent/received requests), chat, profile with resume/experience, availability, goals, Open Door, privacy settings.
- Guest and signed-in modes; ID/email verification states; report, block, mute; AI assistant.
- Must keep Expo Go compatibility.

## Brand Commitments

- Rich purple accent (#5B35C9, the app icon and splash purple) on cool neutral surfaces, used only for actions, selection and meaningful highlights; Bricolage Grotesque headings.
- Personality: calm, trustworthy, useful.

## Evidence on Hand

Demo people and events exist for exploration and must always be clearly labelled as demo. Never present demo data as real users.

## Product Principles

- Privacy first: approximate location, explicit visibility controls, nothing shared without consent.
- Truthful by default: counts, distances and labels reflect real data; demo is labelled.
- Useful over flashy: every screen helps the user find or reach someone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1 body, 3:1 UI), 44pt / 48dp touch targets, screen-reader labels and roles, large-text support.
